# Product Guidelines: Chatwoot

## Tone and Voice
- **Professional but Approachable:** Maintain a helpful and professional tone that reflects the platform's reliability while remaining accessible and friendly.
- **Clear and Direct:** Use concise language to provide clarity in instructions and error messages, ensuring agents can resolve issues quickly.
- **Empowering:** Communicate in a way that makes users feel confident and in control of their customer interactions.

## UX Principles
- **Efficiency First:** Design workflows to minimize the number of clicks required for common agent tasks (e.g., responding to messages, tagging conversations).
- **Accessibility:** Ensure the interface is accessible to all users, following WCAG standards for color contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen reader compatibility.
- **Consistency:** Maintain consistent UI elements and interactions across all channels and modules to reduce cognitive load.
- **Feedback Loops:** Provide immediate and clear feedback for user actions (e.g., successful message sent, error notifications).

## Visual Style
- **Clean and Modern:** Use a clean, uncluttered layout with ample white space to prioritize content (conversations).
- **Subtle Branding:** Incorporate branding elements subtly, focusing on a professional color palette and typography that enhances readability.
- **Responsive Design:** Ensure the dashboard and customer-facing elements provide a seamless experience across desktop and mobile devices.

## Performance and Reliability
- **Responsive Interactions:** Prioritize performance to ensure that message delivery and dashboard updates are near real-time.
- **Robustness:** Design features to handle high concurrency and large volumes of customer data without compromising stability.
- **Data Integrity:** Implement safeguards to ensure customer and conversation data are accurately captured and securely stored.
